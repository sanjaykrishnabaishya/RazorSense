# Zomato Food Delivery Policy

1. **Late Delivery**:
   - If an order is delayed by more than 30 minutes past the estimated time of arrival (ETA), the customer is eligible for a ₹100 Zomato credits coupon.
   - For delays exceeding 60 minutes, the customer can request a full refund, provided they reject the order upon arrival.

2. **Missing Items**:
   - If items are missing from the order, a partial refund corresponding to the missing items' value will be initiated to the original payment source immediately.

3. **Delivery Partner Misbehavior**:
   - We maintain a strict zero-tolerance policy against misbehavior. Any such report must be escalated to human support for immediate investigation.
