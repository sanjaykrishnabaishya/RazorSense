# Amazon Refund and Replacement Policy

1. **Electronics & Gadgets**: 
   - 7 Days Replacement only. No refunds are allowed for electronics unless the item is completely dead on arrival (DOA).
   - If a product is damaged during transit, the customer must upload a photo of the damaged box within 48 hours of delivery.

2. **Apparel & Fashion**:
   - 30 Days Return & Refund. Products must have original tags intact.

3. **Wrong Item Received**:
   - If a customer receives a wrong item, they are eligible for an immediate full refund or a free replacement. The wrong item must be returned to the delivery executive.
